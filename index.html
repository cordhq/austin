FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about ordering, delivery and choosing the right solar setup for your home or business.

What does SunergyX sell?

We supply complete solar power equipment: combo deals, solar panels, inverters, batteries, charge controllers, breakers and protection, energy saving appliances (including solar street lights), and accessories such as solar generators, security cameras and power banks. Use the Products Directory above to browse each category.

How do I know what size inverter and battery I need?

Use our free Estimation Tool. Enter the appliances you run at home (lights, fans, TV, fridge, AC, pump) and it recommends an inverter size, battery capacity and number of panels. For a bigger home, a business, or a setup the tool does not cover, message us and we will size it with you.

How do I pay for my order?

Add items to your cart, enter your name, email and phone number, choose your delivery location and pay securely with Paystack in Naira. Your order details are sent to us on WhatsApp once payment goes through.

What happens after I place an order?

We confirm your order with you on WhatsApp, arrange delivery or pickup, and keep you updated until it reaches you. If anything about your order needs adjusting, we will tell you before dispatch.

How much is delivery?

Delivery fees depend on your location and are shown in the cart when you choose your delivery area.

Can I pick up my order from your store?

Yes, pickup is free. Collect from our store at 18 Sarah Faboyede Street, Bucknor Ejigbo, Lagos. Select Pickup at Store in the cart at checkout.

Do the products come with a warranty?

Yes. Warranty terms differ by product and brand, so the cover for batteries, inverters and panels is not the same. Ask us on WhatsApp before you order and we will confirm the warranty for the exact item you want.

I cannot find the product I need. What should I do?

Our Combo Deals and Energy Saving Appliances ranges are still being expanded, and we can source items that are not listed yet. Tell us what you need on WhatsApp and we will get back to you with options and prices.

Still have a question?Message our team on WhatsApp and we will help you.
